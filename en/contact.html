Contact

Found a bug, have a suggestion, or want to talk about a collaboration — this page lists the right channel for each.

How to reach us

Either channel works — pick whichever is easier:

  • Email: yangzixiongh@163.com — best for long descriptions or anything needing an attachment;
  • GitHub: github.com/yangzixiong — messages and suggestions are equally welcome.

ImageLab has no backend and no on-site message board — contact happens only through the two channels above. You never fill in or submit anything on this site.

Reply time

Replies usually arrive within 1-2 business days; holidays may take a little longer. For how-to questions, the FAQ may already have the answer.

What to include in a report

To keep the back-and-forth short, include as much of the following as applies:

  1. the URL of the page where it happened (copy it straight from the address bar);
  2. your browser and operating system versions;
  3. the steps you took, plus any error text or screenshot you saw;
  4. if a specific image is involved, its format (e.g. 16-bit TIFF) and origin — send the file itself by email only when a sample is really needed.

Other entry points

Project background is on the About page; anything about data and logs is covered in the Privacy page; more detail on feedback channels is also in the FAQ.
