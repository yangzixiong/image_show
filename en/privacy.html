Privacy

One page covering what this site cannot touch, what it stores, and what ends up in the logs — every claim checkable for yourself.

Images never leave the browser

From the moment you pick a file, it is handled locally: FileReader or URL.createObjectURL reads it in the browser, then it is decoded and drawn on the canvas. The scripts contain no fetch and no XMLHttpRequest — there is no code path that could upload an image. Nothing is sent to this site's server, and nothing is sent to any third party.

What the server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Your images generate no upload requests at all, but every page view is recorded by Cloudflare as routine access logs — IP, User-Agent, timestamps and similar — for security and traffic statistics, kept under the host's own retention policy. Those logs never contain your image content.

About advertising

There are currently no ads on this site and no advertising cookies are set. If Google AdSense is added later, Google and its partners may use cookies for ad data collection; this page will be updated to explain the use, with a link to the Google advertising privacy policy kept here for reference. The current content of this page is the source of truth for ad status.
