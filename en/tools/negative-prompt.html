Negative Prompt Builder

Four groups of common negative phrases: tick them and they join the preview on the right, with duplicates across groups removed automatically. Fill in the positive prompt too and copy the whole pair straight into A1111 or ComfyUI.

Library (tick to add to the negative prompt)
Custom additions (comma-separated, appended at the end)
Negative prompt (live preview)
Positive prompt (for copying the full pair)

Which group to pick

General quality is the all-rounder: tick lowres, compression artefacts, watermarks and text in one go and most failures are covered. Pose & anatomy keeps bodies correct — always tick it for people. Anti-anime is for realistic output: putting anime, cartoon and line art in the negative is what stops results drifting into illustration style. SDXL hands targets six fingers and fused palms — reach for it first whenever hands come out wrong.

Deduplication and order

The output follows library order, and an entry that appears in two ticked groups is merged case-insensitively — tick bad hands in both groups and it still shows up once. Custom text is split on commas, deduplicated line by line and appended last; newlines count as separators too, so you can paste a whole word list straight in.

Using the positive/negative pair

The positive box starts at masterpiece, best quality — replace it with your main prompt and hit Copy full pair to get a blank-line-separated structure: top half into A1111's positive field, bottom half into the negative field. Paste that same structure back into the prompt splitter to verify the round trip in one click.
