Why nothing is uploaded

The file never leaves your machine. The browser hands it to a local blob URL, <video> decodes it, and a canvas copies the pixels out. Turn off Wi-Fi and the page still works — there is no request to make.

How to enter timestamps

Timestamps are plain seconds and may be fractional: 0.5, 1.2, 7.5, separated by commas or newlines. Interval mode starts at 0 and steps forward until it hits “Max frames”, so start small, check the look, then raise the cap.

Quality and file size

A lossless PNG of one 1080p frame is often over 2 MB. JPEG and WebP come out an order of magnitude smaller — the quality slider only applies to those two. Dropping the output width to 1280 or 854 is plenty for reference sheets and thumbnails. If a file refuses to decode it is usually HEVC, AV1 or ProRes; remux to H.264 MP4 or WebM first.
