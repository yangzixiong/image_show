Where these ratios fit

1:1 for avatars and product shots, 9:16 for phone screens and story covers, 16:9 for video and desktop wallpapers, 3:2 / 2:3 close to camera originals. Stable Diffusion 1.5 works best around 512×512 and 512×768, SDXL around 1024 — pick the ratio, set the long side, and you have a ready canvas.

About lossless

With “Original pixels” and PNG, cropping only moves pixels — no resampling, no re-compression, truly lossless. Choosing 512 / 768 / 1024 applies one high-quality resize; JPEG and WebP are lossy but smaller, and the quality slider only affects those two formats.

Touch friendly

The box and its eight handles use pointer events, so they drag the same way with a finger on phones and tablets. The dimmed area is what gets cut; the output size updates live in the side panel.
