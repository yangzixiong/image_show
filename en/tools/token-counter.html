Prompt Token Counter

Why CLIP counts 77 per segment

The text encoder behind SD1.5 and SDXL consumes at most 77 tokens per pass, and two of them are special tokens, leaving 75 for your words. Longer prompts are split on BREAK and encoded segment by segment, so this page counts per segment as x/77 and highlights any segment that goes over — truncated content is exactly the tail of the prompt, where the least important ideas are supposed to sit.

Four tokenizers, four answers

The same sentence has different token counts under different models: CLIP uses its own BPE vocabulary; T5-XXL uses SentencePiece with a 512 limit in SD3; o200k_base and cl100k_base are OpenAI tiktoken encodings used by GPT-4o and GPT-3.5/4 respectively. Switching models swaps the vocabulary and recounts. CLIP and both tiktoken tables are exact — results match the official implementations.

When vocabularies load

All four vocabularies live in the repository under data/tok/ with no CDN involved; they are fetched only when this page opens or when you switch models, so every other page stays untouched. Until the vocabulary is ready the number carries a ≈ prefix and is an estimate; it becomes exact the moment loading finishes. T5’s SentencePiece vocabulary is not bundled yet, so that model always shows the estimate and says so in the status line.
