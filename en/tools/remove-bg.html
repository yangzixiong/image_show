Background Removal & Cutout Masks

A neural network runs inside your browser to separate the foreground: MODNet for portraits, U2Netp for objects. You get two results — a transparent PNG and a grayscale mask ready for ControlNet. The image never leaves your machine.

Transparent cutout (checkerboard = transparency)
Grayscale mask (ControlNet ready)
Load an image, then press “Remove background”

Model

Portrait model ≈ 6.3 MB
Object model ≈ 4.4 MB
Models download on first use

Process

Slow once, fast after

The inference engine (~11 MB) and the model download only when you first press the button; the browser caches them afterwards. Inference itself runs locally: a few seconds on a modern machine, possibly longer on an old one. Inputs are automatically resized to what the model needs, which keeps things fast.

Which model to pick

Person in the photo? Pick Portrait — MODNet is matting-focused and noticeably better on hair edges. Products, animals, still objects? Pick Object — U2Netp is a general saliency segmenter that handles anything, with slightly rougher edges. When in doubt, run both and keep the better result.

The mask is ControlNet-ready

In the grayscale mask, white marks the foreground to keep and black the background — exactly the format ControlNet (seg / scribble controls) expects, no post-processing needed. The transparent PNG is for compositing onto new backgrounds.
