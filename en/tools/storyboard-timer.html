Storyboard Timer

Write one line per shot with a duration, hit play, and the clock runs in real time with a beep at every cut. Check the total against 15 / 30 / 60 seconds, then copy the board or export it as CSV.

Space bar plays and pauses. Durations can be fractional, e.g. 2.5.

Why time a storyboard at all

Every script says “this shot is about three seconds” until you watch it and find out it is five. Generative video makes it worse: default clip lengths are usually longer than you expect. Pick a target length, fill in the seconds, and anything over budget turns red the moment you type it.

How to use it

Add shots until you have the right count, then edit durations and one-line descriptions. “Split evenly” divides the target length across the current shots — handy for a first draft. Press play: the clock follows real time, the current shot is highlighted, finished ones grey out, and a tone marks each cut (you can turn it off).

Where the export goes

“Copy board” gives plain text that survives a paste into Notion, Slack or a script file. “Export CSV” adds a BOM so Excel opens the Chinese text without mojibake; the columns are shot, seconds, description.
